# Privacy Policy for TopNotch

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

TopNotch ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when you use the TopNotch desktop application for Windows.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
TopNotch is designed with a strict **local-first, offline-first** philosophy:
- We **do not collect, store, or transmit** any personal identifiable information (PII).
- We **do not track** your usage, clicks, keystrokes, or application behavior.
- We **do not maintain** any remote servers, databases, or analytics services that receive your data.

### 2. Local-Only Storage
All data associated with TopNotch—including your pinned notes, shelf files, clipboard history, and user settings—is stored **strictly and exclusively on your local computer**. None of this data is ever sent across the network.

### 3. Network and Internet Access
TopNotch does not transmit your personal data over the internet. Any internet requests performed (such as checking for updates via the Microsoft Store) are handled natively and securely through official Windows and Microsoft Store APIs without exposing your private information.

### 4. Third-Party Services
TopNotch does not integrate with any third-party analytics, telemetry, or advertising SDKs. No third party receives information about you or your device through our software.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because TopNotch does not collect any personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children or any other user demographic.

### 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
